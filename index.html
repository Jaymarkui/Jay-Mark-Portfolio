About me

Hi, I’m Jay-Mark Gallardo, Currently 4th year student studying BSCOE at ICCT Colleges. I’m a UI/UX designer who also works in DevOps.

As a UI/UX designer, I design clear, easy-to-use screens that people can understand quickly, from the first sketch to a finished interface.

In DevOps, I work on the tools and automation that build, test, and release software so it runs reliably for the people using it.

My Experience through Project

The projects I made for study purpose in ICCT Cainta

  1. 2024

    Library Management

    ICCT Colleges

    I made this project to help a library manage its books and records in one place instead of on paper. Paper records take time to search and are easy to lose or mix up. The purpose of the system is to save the librarian’s time and keep the library’s information organized and easy to find.

  2. 2025

    AlertNet PH

    ICCT Colleges · Add school year

    We made AlertNet PH because many people in the Philippines are affected by typhoons, floods, and other disasters, and they need to know what is coming and what to do. Our purpose was to put disaster alerts, emergency contacts, and preparedness tips in one easy-to-use place so people can get ready and stay safe. It also has guides, a map, and a quiz to help people learn how to prepare.
